About SEA MASSAGE & HEAD SPA

A calm place to slow down in Phuket Old Town.

SEA Massage & Head Spa puts the head spa at the heart of the visit. Shirodhara, where warm herbal oil is poured slowly over the forehead, and Vietnamese head therapy, with pressure-point work on the scalp and neck. We choose natural products with care for your hair and scalp.

Beyond the head spa, you will find Thai, foot and hot stone massage, facial massage, body scrubs and rituals, and a menu made for kids. Open every day, 10:00–23:00 — book online or message us on WhatsApp.
